About OSISA

Working towards open, democratic and inclusive societies across Southern Africa.

Who We Are

OSISA is committed to supporting open societies, democracy, human rights and social justice across Southern Africa.

We work with communities, civil society organisations and other partners to encourage participation, equality and accountable institutions.
